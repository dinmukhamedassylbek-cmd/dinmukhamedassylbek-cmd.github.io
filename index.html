High revisit time InSAR Intelligent Analysis for Near Real Time Monitoring of Ground Dynamics

The research direction combines Sentinel-1 SAR processing, interferometry, time-series analysis and intelligent interpretation of deformation signals. The long-term goal is to reduce the gap between satellite acquisition and actionable deformation information.
