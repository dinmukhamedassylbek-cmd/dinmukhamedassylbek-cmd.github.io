About

Research focused on turning complex measurements into useful information.

My background combines electronics, communication technologies, signal/data processing and geospatial analysis. I prefer practical workflows in which raw measurements become interpretable and reproducible results.

My current research direction is intelligent InSAR analysis with short-revisit SAR data for near-real-time monitoring of ground dynamics.

Active research direction

High revisit time InSAR Intelligent Analysis for Near Real Time Monitoring of Ground Dynamics

The research direction combines Sentinel-1 SAR processing, interferometry, time-series analysis and intelligent interpretation of deformation signals. The long-term goal is to reduce the gap between satellite acquisition and actionable deformation information.
